# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Fish Pond to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, sustained disruption of discussions or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation for people who break it.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/AftabIbrahimKazi/fish-pond/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer, [Aftab Ibrahim Kazi](https://github.com/AftabIbrahimKazi). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
